# Independent editorial review for the week ending November 4

The mathematics pilot collected 559 papers submitted October 29 through November 4. We reviewed all nine ranked groups under the criteria established on November 19, plus ten exclusions selected by the audit. We checked for three papers on one question and for accurate dates, assumptions and conclusions. A failed quotation check left an exclusion pending. ## Rechecking failed quotations

Of the same 73 candidates, 69 earlier decisions were reused and four were retried for an additional $0.0023. Each retry returned a verified passage, preserving all nine ranks. In the tail-estimation group only two shared the question. In the difference-set group the three companions studied primes and partitions. All ten original exclusions were supported after retry. The previous judgments on the ranked summaries stand. [Rerun and ledger](https://github.com/jam-sudo/TrendBio/actions/runs/44595121475)
