Only BRUMM budgets the time to reclaim a process group’s memory. MPORA changes cache and memory-bandwidth allocations and measures how often tasks meet deadlines. Its uncertainty estimates have a probabilistic coverage guarantee. PREEMPT-FaaS measures orchestration and service latency under load. Its sixfold improvement is a comparison with the baseline controller on the tested workloads. Each team measured a different operation.
