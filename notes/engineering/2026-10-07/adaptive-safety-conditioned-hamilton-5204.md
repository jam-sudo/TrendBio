The navigation study measures mission completion and tracking error under disturbances. These are empirical results. The vehicle study tests lateral containment, and the model-bank study gives a safety guarantee conditional on retaining an appropriate model. The three hardware demonstrations support different claims about operating under changing conditions. [Navigation study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03530v1) · [Vehicle containment study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08115v1) · [Model-bank study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08765v1)
