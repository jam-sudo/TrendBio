The recycled-aggregate study defines its leading predictor as water mass divided by cement mass. It did not have detailed binder composition for all 407 mixtures. The other two papers use total binder in the denominator. Two studies predict compressive strength, and the printed-concrete study predicts flexural strength. The ratios rank first within their respective models.
