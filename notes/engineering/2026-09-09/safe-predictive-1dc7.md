The decentralized shielding paper reports simulation experiments in Section 7. Its abstract describes the design and omits those experiments. The occlusion study proves collision avoidance under its stated assumptions and tests hardware. The third paper reports fewer violations in a racing simulation and a crash-free model-car trial. Each result has its own test conditions.
