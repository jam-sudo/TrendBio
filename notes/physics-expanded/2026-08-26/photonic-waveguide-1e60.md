Two studies also report measurements. The waveguide team measured transmission through fabricated subwavelength gratings as a function of geometry. The micro-ring team used integrated heaters to vary the relative input phase and measured the output spectrum of a fabricated silicon device. The woodpile study computed transmission as a function of twist.
