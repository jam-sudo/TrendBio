The first study models a proto-quark star made of strange quark matter throughout. The other two studies model hybrid stars with hadronic matter and examine the conditions for a quark phase. All three calculate how the equation of state affects the maximum stellar mass. [Strange-quark stars](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.06024v1) · [Proto-hybrid stars](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09195) · [Cold hybrid stars](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12653)
