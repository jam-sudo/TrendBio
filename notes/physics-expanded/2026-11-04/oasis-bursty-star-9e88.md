The 29% estimate in OASIS I applies to galaxies at redshift 4–5.5 with stellar masses of 100 million to about 3 billion solar masses, in survey groups selected independently of star formation activity. OASIS II inferred how long suppression lasts. OASIS III measured how recent star formation varies with galaxy size.
