The modular-memory simulation reports at least a tenfold reduction in qubit overhead and projects more than thirtyfold for larger codes. The yoked-code simulation finds 4–4.5 times the memory density at its stated error target. The geometry study doubles code distance while holding the qubit count and encoding rate fixed. Its fourfold efficiency gain refers to that distance-based metric.
