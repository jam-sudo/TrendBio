The tau-pair study projects precision on entanglement and Bell-sensitive observables. The polarized baryon study uses decay angles to constrain short-distance couplings. The top-quark study uses bottom-quark and W-boson spin correlations to project limits on anomalous couplings. Each projection refers to a different observable and facility.
