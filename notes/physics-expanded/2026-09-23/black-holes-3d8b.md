Three studies use Event Horizon Telescope observations of M87* and Sgr A* to assess their models. Two report bounds on individual model parameters. The Kalb–Ramond study also compares the observed diameters with combinations of spin, viewing angle and deformation parameters. Its illustrative values for one deformation parameter assume fixed values for the others.
