The two little-red-dot analogues have estimated dust reservoirs of roughly 100 to 1,000 solar masses. Their photometry permits pre-existing dust, so that mass is an upper limit on how much the proposed outflow could have supplied. The red-supergiant calculations predict condensation in stellar winds, and the Wolf–Rayet estimate concerns one observed outburst. Both the physical objects and the methods differ.
