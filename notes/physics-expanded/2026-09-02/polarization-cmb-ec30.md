BICEP3 measured its detector beams in the field. The authors used those measurements as inputs to timestream simulations and estimated the remaining leakage after deprojection. The quoted value is a preliminary simulation result informed by measured beams. [BICEP3 beam measurements and leakage analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25016v1)
