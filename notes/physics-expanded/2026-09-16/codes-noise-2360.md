The three models use different noise channels. The ZX and ZY surface-code study varies the bias of Pauli-X errors and adds correlated XX crosstalk in a separate set of simulations. The honeycomb Floquet study models leakage. The five-qubit study evaluates generalized amplitude damping with post-selected recovery.
