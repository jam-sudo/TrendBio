All three studies quantify effects in simulations. The redshift-distribution study finds shifts of −1.7σ in σ8 and +1.4σ in Ωm for one LSST-like setup. The intrinsic-alignment paper reports biases up to about 12σ in some 15,000-square-degree simulations. The blending paper finds that changing the definition of overlap reduces the tension between two parameter contours from 1.6σ to 0.54σ. The studies use different survey assumptions and comparisons.
