Basile Gallet explicitly builds Part II on the equations he derived with Alexandre Tlili in Part I. The reduced surface-wave amplitude depends on two horizontal coordinates and couples to a three-dimensional background flow. Part II verifies conservation of wave action, energy and horizontal momentum for the inviscid model. The paper leaves numerical studies for future work.
