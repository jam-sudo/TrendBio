Theorem 1.2 gives an algorithm for sparse mixed states of rank above one with polynomial running time in the number of copies and qubits. The open question is whether a faster algorithm can approach the time needed to read those copies. The authors give nearly optimal copy complexity for mixed states and nearly optimal copy and time bounds for pure states. [Theorem 1.2 and the open question](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.12219v1#S1)
