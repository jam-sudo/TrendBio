The three analyses use different sources of calibration. Zheng and colleagues combine DESI with supernovae and cosmic chronometers. Paopiamsap and colleagues add an early-universe baryon-density prior from Big Bang nucleosynthesis to their angular BAO measurements. Zhang and colleagues use fast radio bursts and derive their result without that prior. [Supernova and chronometer analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08836v1) · [Angular BAO analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10215v1) · [Fast radio burst analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.15536v1)
