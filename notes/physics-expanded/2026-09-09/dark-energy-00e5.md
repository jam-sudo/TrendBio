The model comparison depends on the coupling and the statistic. The emergent dark-energy and constant-coupling studies report Bayesian evidence favoring ΛCDM. The conformally coupled study finds a lower minimum chi-squared for its two coupled models and reports support under the Akaike information criterion for many data combinations.
