The hierarchical sampling paper specifies its assumptions in Theorem 4.4. It compares samplers at the same target accuracy, uses a geometric schedule for intermediate solver accuracy and requires a positive bound that holds across tempering stages. The authors measured the 14.3-fold speedup in one photoacoustic imaging experiment. [Theorem 4.4 and experiments](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.37082v1#S4)
