The surveys measured residents’ knowledge, attitudes and reported experiences. The Zimbabwe study examined legal knowledge about ostriches. In Pakistan, respondents who reported more wolves killed in their community were more likely to express negative views after adjustment for livestock injury and other factors. The Ethiopian study related attitudes to local conditions and herd size. The studies used different questionnaires and did not track subsequent wildlife populations or the effects of conservation measures.
