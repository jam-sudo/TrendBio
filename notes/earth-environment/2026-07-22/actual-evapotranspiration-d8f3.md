Six of the seven Australian model-validation sites also appear in the satellite study. The two site tables list Dry River, Gingin, Litchfield, Robson Creek, Tumbarumba and Wombat Forest at matching locations. The studies also share a coauthor, Keirnan Fowler. The South African study used a separate weighing lysimeter.
