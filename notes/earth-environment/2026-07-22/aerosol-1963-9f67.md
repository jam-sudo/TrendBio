Two teams used the same CALIOP satellite data release for different analyses. Kloss and colleagues initialized their Hunga plume transport model with version 4.51 lidar observations. Martinsson and colleagues used that release to estimate stratospheric aerosol over 2006–2023, including Hunga. The historical Agung profiles come from ground-based searchlight observations in New Mexico.
