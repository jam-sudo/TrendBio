The Peruvian study describes the puma as Least Concern globally and Near Threatened in Peru. It estimated density from camera records. The Indian study tracked florican movements and the French study estimated toad survival and recruitment. The three studies inform local conservation using different outcomes. [Peruvian puma study](https://doi.org/10.3389/fcosc.2026.1895819) · [Indian florican study](https://doi.org/10.3389/fcosc.2026.1911080) · [French toad study](https://doi.org/10.3897/natureconservation.64.202089)
