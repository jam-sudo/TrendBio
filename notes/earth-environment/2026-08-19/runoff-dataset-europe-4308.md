EARLS used E-OBS weather observations to train its runoff model. BiasCast used E-OBS in its reanalysis baseline, alongside other weather products. This establishes shared inputs. An exact match of the training gauges or dates would require checking the individual records. The warmer-climate study used 918 catchments in central Europe and shares a coauthor, Corinna Frank, with EARLS.
