The studies describe three distinct products. One pairs ocean temperature and salinity profiles with satellite-tracked eddies. Another assimilates local water measurements in a model of Ise Bay. The third reconstructs offshore winds using satellite winds and marine observations. Whether any underlying observations appear in more than one product remains unverified.
