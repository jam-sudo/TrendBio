The Ecuadorian study compared students’ own ratings of practicum documentation across four cohorts. The systematic review examined evidence on intercultural preparation. The bibliometric study mapped publications on digital and media literacy. These studies observed different outcomes, and none directly tested changes in classroom teaching performance.
