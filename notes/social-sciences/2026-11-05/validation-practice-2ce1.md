The US school-staff feasibility survey reported internal consistency and explored associations between ratings of the school environment. The Spanish, Peruvian and Italian student studies also tested the internal structure of their instruments. The four surveys measure different constructs. [Staff feasibility survey](https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1856108) · [Inclusive practice scale](https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1903785) · [Adolescent stress questionnaire](https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1978642) · [School behaviour measure](https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1993233)
