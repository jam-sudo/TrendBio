The bismuth study compares bandgaps across three halides and reports broadband emission for the iodide film. The zincophosphite study changes the emission color by adjusting the ratio of adsorbed europium and terbium. The phosphonium study measures how the emission changes when solvent exchange moves iodide within the crystal.
