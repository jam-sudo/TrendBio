## Calibration method and coverage

Read-only replay of 60 daily seven-day windows from 2026-09-07 through 2026-11-05 (inclusive), with six earlier lookback days. Inputs: committed `data/computational/items` and float16 shards in `data/computational/embeddings`; no embeddings generated, no LLM calls, no historical files changed. Relevant papers only; exclude a preprint when its published counterpart is in the window; complete-linkage cosine distance 0.10 and minimum size 3. Average stored float32 paper vectors and L2-normalize the centroid; compare each normalized code/industry vector against every centroid and retain its best cluster per day. This matches the daily implementation.

Repeated items are intentional: daily centroid drift can change the best cluster and whether the item clears the cutoff. The table records the 40 item/day pairs nearest 0.94, not 40 independent examples. Titles are the three paper members nearest that item, sorted by cosine. Judgments are manual semantic review of the titles and item descriptions; this small sample does not establish a population precision rate.

## Enrichment diagnosis

Sync enrichment previously allowed only 512 output tokens, versus 1,024 in batch. It recorded `empty_output` when the SDK returned no parsed output, then discarded `stop_reason` and usage details. There are no saved response bodies or stop metadata in the historical item records, so the six Nov 5 empties and two root validation failures cannot be definitively attributed retrospectively without a new live call (prohibited).

The supported mechanism is token exhaustion before JSON: [Anthropic's Haiku 5.5 migration guide](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/models/haiku-5-5/migration-guide#configure-thinking) confirms adaptive thinking defaults on, consumes `max_tokens`, and can stop after thinking before text. The 512-versus-1,024 discrepancy is consistent with the reported higher sync failure rate, but is not proof of each historical failure. New warnings include `stop_reason` and `usage.output_tokens`; a mocked `max_tokens` response proves one retry with the limit raised from 512 to 1,024, identical prompt and low effort. Empty output and all `validation:*` failures retry once; refusals, grounding failures and exhausted HTTP retries do not get this extra retry. Code/industry fallbacks are irrelevant; paper fallback behavior and `enrichment-fallback:` notes remain.

## Site and compatibility

The Industry category chip is removed when its topic count is zero. This is the simplest option: no added scrolling/focus state, and existing keyboard behavior is retained. “From industry” is an independent, labelled section below Top 40 on home/day routes, newest first by first-seen date, at most eight relevant industry items in that day's inclusive seven-day window. RSS files are loaded once per process, not all paper records on every route. Existing BACKFILLED notices remain unchanged.

`TopicDay.attached_item_ids` defaults to empty for old files. Paper IDs alone drive centroid, count, rise, volume and Jaccard continuity. All evidence drives diversity/star signals and synthesis; topic pages and synthesis repair read attached IDs too. New attachments invalidate the same-day synthesis cache. Period unique-item counts remain paper-based, consistent with historical counts.
